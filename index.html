AGENDA

What we're covering today.

01
What do we want to learn?

What questions are you actually trying to answer? What would change — about your business, your product, your messaging — if you had this data?

02
Who are we asking?

Current customers, prospective ones, both? How we reach them shapes everything from platform choice to question framing.

03
What info do we want to capture?

Email addresses are on the table. What else? Demographics, purchase intent, brand perception? Let's map it out before we write a single question.

04
What's the tradeoff for taking it?

People are busy. What do they get for 5 minutes of their time? Discount, early access, a result, a give-back? We need an honest answer here.

05
How does it get in front of people?

Email list, social, in-store, partner channels? Distribution isn't an afterthought — it shapes format, length, and tone.

OUR THINKING

Where our heads are at going in.

We're not starting cold. Based on what we know so far, a few things are already clear. We want to stress-test these assumptions today.

The goal isn't data — it's decisions.

  • → Every question should connect to a real choice you'll make
  • → If the answer won't change anything, cut the question
  • → Short surveys get completed. Long ones get abandoned.

Email capture needs a real reason.

  • → "Sign up for updates" isn't a reason
  • → A discount, a result, or exclusive access is
  • → The reward has to feel proportional to the ask

The survey and the promo are one thing.

  • → How you talk about the survey is part of the design
  • → The headline that gets people to click matters as much as Q1
  • → We're designing both at the same time

Platform follows purpose.

  • → No platform recommendation until we know what this needs to do
  • → Logic branching, visual design, embed options — it all depends
  • → After today, we'll have enough to make a real call

After this meeting: we build the questions, recommend a platform, then design the survey and the promotional piece.
