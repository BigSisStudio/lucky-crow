Week 1 · Wednesday
Step into the Crow's Nest — Apothecary Explainer
Instagram Facebook
luckycrowmadison
Madison, Wisconsin
VIDEO FPO
luckycrowmadison Step into the Crow's Nest with me. First up — what does Apothecary even mean, and why is it part of our name?
@luckycrowmadison @lotusenergydrinks
Script
Hi, I'm Katy, and I'm building a space in Madison called Lucky Crow Apothecary & Readeasy. Every so often I'm going to invite you into the Crow's Nest with me, to share a little more about what we're up to, why we're doing certain things, and some fun reveals along the way to opening.

Let's start with the name, and in particular the Apothecary part. Lucky Crow is actually two spaces in one. There's the apothecary, and there's the Readeasy, which we'll get to in another post.

I wanted "apothecary" in there for a reason. Old apothecaries were all about wellness, tonics, and little elixirs made to help you feel good. That's exactly what we're going for, and why plant-based energy drinks are a big part of what we'll offer. Treats for your body and mind that are different than everything else out there.

That's the first peek. More to come soon.

luckycrowmadison Behind every small business is a unique founder story, so here's a little more about my journey to Lucky Crow. I'll be sharing more along the way as we get closer to opening in Madison, so check back for more about the origins of what I'm building.
@luckycrowmadison @lotusenergydrinks
Script
Hi, I'm Katy, and before deciding to open Lucky Crow, I spent almost twenty years working for other people, most recently as a web developer here in Madison.

Lucky Crow embodies a lot of what I've learned along the way. Working and interacting with people is something I've always loved, and this is the place that brings that together with delicious plant-based energy drinks and a comfortable place for people to get lost in a book, relax, or get some work done. Can't wait to show you more and tell you about how I landed on the name and look of Lucky Crow. See you soon.
Week 1 · Friday
You Said, We Listened — Later Hours
Facebook Instagram
luckycrowmadison
Madison, Wisconsin
VIDEO FPO
luckycrowmadison We're getting a lot of questions about Lucky Crow and what to expect when we open. So, we're going to start answering some of them here. If you have a question, leave a comment and I'll do my best to get to it in a future post.
@luckycrowmadison
Script
We haven't even opened yet, but a lot of interested people are asking a lot of interesting questions. Starting today, I'll be hopping on here occasionally to answer some of those questions. Let's start with an easy one.

(Katy opens a piece of paper and reads) "So many places around town close too early! What are your hours?" You're right! A lot of spots around Madison close in the late afternoon. We wanted to provide a space that offered more flexibility, because everyone's day is different and the night owls need a place to land, too. So, we'll be open from 7:00am - 8:00pm, every day.

Got a question? Drop it in the comments and check back for more answers.
